# Post-Loss Protocol

**This is a sequence, not a suggestion. It runs after every loss, including small ones.**
`/tilt` executes this file step by step.

The loss is not the problem. The next ten minutes are the problem.

## Step 2 — Write it down before anything else

I must write, in today's journal, before I look at another chart:

1. Which setup was it, by name?
2. Was the loss a **rule-following loss** or a **rule-breaking loss**? (These are completely different events. Only one of them is a problem.)
3. What exactly invalidated it?
4. Would I take that same trade again with the same information? Yes or no.
5. What was I feeling in the 60 seconds before I clicked?

## Step 3 — The honesty questions

Answer out loud, not in my head:

- Am I trying to get back to flat? <!-- if yes: done for the day -->
- Am I looking at a lower timeframe than my plan allows? <!-- if yes: done for the day -->
- Am I about to size up? <!-- if yes: done for the day -->
- Am I hunting for a setup instead of waiting for one? <!-- if yes: done for the day -->
- Am I annoyed at the market, at myself, or at nothing in particular?
